Our story

From a roadside stall to a proper table

Raavi Chai House began in 2015 as a single stall near the Ravi bank. Our founder, Bilal Raza, wanted tea that tasted like his grandmother's: strong, sweet and unhurried.

Today we serve students, families and night-shift workers. We still buy leaf directly from growers and boil every pot to order.
